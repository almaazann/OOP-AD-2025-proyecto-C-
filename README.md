# OOP-AD-2025-proyecto-C-
The program manages a hospital. Imagine you arrive at a clinic. At reception, someone registers you—they record your name and age and open a file containing your medical history. That file belongs to you; it's created with you as a patient, and if you're ever removed from the system, it disappears with it.
Then you're assigned a doctor. That doctor isn't "yours"—they exist in the clinic independently of you, they see many patients, and if you leave, they're still there.
To see you, an appointment is scheduled. That appointment connects a patient with a doctor on a specific date and time. When the appointment ends and is deleted from the system, the doctor and the patient continue to exist separately.
The clinic also has staff beyond the doctors—nurses, administrators—all are "clinic staff," but each performs different tasks. The clinic encompasses them all, and when the clinic closes, all of that staff disappears with it.
Finally, there are resources like equipment and medications, which also belong to the clinic.
